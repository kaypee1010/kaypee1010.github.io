    DOCUMENT 06 · VERSION 1.0 DRAFT

    Consent form and information sheet

    Organised by the R&D Cell and the Center of AI, SGT University, Gurugram. Convenor and data contact: Kamal Preet Singh. This sheet follows the principles of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023: we tell you the purpose, collect only what we need, keep it only as long as needed, you can withdraw, and you have a named person to contact.

    1. What this championship is

    One Case. Your School. Your Rules. is a student championship built on a fictional case, The Neemgarh File. You take a 12-minute phone qualifier (Episode 0: Open the File), then compete in teams through four episodes, ending in a stage finale. Some of this is recorded. This sheet is about those recordings and your written work.

    2. What we collect and why

    • Registration details (name, university registration number, mobile number, email (university or personal), school, year): to register you, check you are a current SGT student, form teams, contact you about the event and issue certificates. Your mobile number is never shown publicly, on leaderboards or to other teams. Needed.
    • Your Episode 0 answers and 120-word note: to score Episode 0. Needed.
    • Your 60-second voice note: to score speaking. It is transcribed and rated by software; a human checks a sample. Needed.
    • Audio and video of spoken arguments, cross-examinations and the finale pitch: to score you, review appeals and give feedback. Needed.
    • Written submissions and prompt ledgers: to score you and check integrity. Needed.
    • Photos and video at venues: for university communications. Optional (box D).
    • Anonymised speech and writing samples: for research on English fluency. Optional (box C).

    We do not collect your Aadhaar number, financial details, caste, religion, health information or home address. If any of these appear in something you write or say, we do not use them.

    3. Who sees your data

    • The organising team (staff and volunteers with a signed confidentiality undertaking): registration data, submissions, recordings, scores.
    • Jurors: your submissions and live performance, by team code only in Episodes 1 to 3; names at the finale.
    • The event platform and the speech-rating service: under a written agreement, only to run the championship.
    • The finale audience and university communications: your finale pitch and photos, only if you tick box D.
    • The convenor's research team: anonymised samples only, if you tick box C.

    4. Your choices

    Box A, recording for scoring (required). Your voice note, spoken arguments, cross-examinations and finale pitch may be recorded (audio and video), transcribed and rated by software and by human jurors, to score you and review any appeal. Identifiable recordings are deleted 12 months after the finale, unless you renew consent.

    Box B, written submissions (required). Your written answers, notes, reports, flaw lists, slides and prompt ledgers may be read and scored, checked for plagiarism and AI disclosure, and kept as a record of the championship. Kept with your name for 12 months after the finale, then deleted or anonymised. Your mobile number and registration number are stored only to contact you about the event and to check you are an eligible SGT student, and follow the same 12 months.

    Box C, research use (optional). The convenor is researching English fluency in university students under university ethics approval. If you agree, your voice note, its transcript, your 120-word note and your written submissions may be used as anonymised samples: your name, email, phone, team name and any identifying words are removed first. Results may be published; none will identify you. Kept up to 5 years. Your participation and scores are not affected by your choice.

    Box D, photos and video (optional). Photos and video of you at venues and on stage may be used on SGT University's website, social media, newsletters, posters and highlight reel for this and future editions. You can ask for any photo or clip to be taken down at any time. Without box D we avoid featuring you, though wide shots of a public stage cannot fully exclude you.

    5. Your rights

    • See or correct your data: email the privacy helpdesk from the email address you registered with; reply within 15 working days.
    • Withdraw box C: any time. Samples are anonymised 90 days after the finale; withdraw before then and nothing of yours enters the research set.
    • Withdraw box D: name the photo, clip or post; taken down within 15 working days.
    • Delete identifiable recordings and submissions (A and B): after the appeal period closes; deletion within 30 days. Scores and certificates stay as records.
    • Complain: first to the convenor, then the university's Grievance Redressal Cell, or the Data Protection Board of India as the Act provides.

    Withdrawing A or B during the championship means you cannot continue to compete, because scoring depends on them.

    6. Security

    Data is stored on the event platform and university systems with access limited to named people. Recordings are named by participant ID and team code wherever possible. We do not sell or share your data with anyone not named above. If a breach affects you, we tell you and the university within 72 hours.

    7. Students under 18

    Your parent or guardian must also sign the form. Email a photo of the signed form to the privacy helpdesk before Episode 0 opens. Your registration is held until we receive it.

    The privacy helpdesk address is published on the event home page.
