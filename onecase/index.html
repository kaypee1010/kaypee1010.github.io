Exhibit 02 / What happened

Twenty-three children. Forty-two acres. One file.

The full case file opens for every registered student in Episode 0: Open the File.

Note to file
Neemgarh / SeptemberFictional
  1. Neemgarh: about 2,400 people, a canal, two borewells, 22 km from campus.
  2. In September, twenty-three children fell ill.
  3. Buffaloes stopped eating. Crops on 42 acres dried up.
  4. Everyone blamed something different. Nobody agreed.
  5. One nursing student posted the right advice on 12 September. Nobody listened for eight days.
  6. The SDM wants answers in 30 days. Twenty SGT schools will each give one.

    Exhibit 08 / Questions

    Before you ask.

    Who can take part?

    Any student currently enrolled at SGT University, from any school and any year: undergraduate, postgraduate or doctoral. The rulebook lists the few exceptions, such as members of the organising team.

    Does it cost anything?

    No. Registration is free.

    Do I need a team to register?

    No. You register alone, in about 60 seconds. Teams of 3 from the same school form after Episode 0, and you invite your two by link.

    What is Open the File?

    A 12-minute qualifier on your phone: 5 reasoning questions, a 120-word note and a 60-second voice note. Take it any time from 4 to 8 January. You need it to join a team.

    Can I use AI tools?

    Yes, where the rules allow it, and you declare it in a prompt ledger. Episode 3 rewards honest, careful use of AI.

    Is Neemgarh a real place?

    No. The village, the company and the people are fictional. Do not contact real officials or companies about the case.

    Which email do I use?

    Any email you check on your phone: your SGT University email or a personal one such as Gmail. We send a 6-digit code there to check it is you. No code after a minute? Look in Spam or Promotions. You also give your university registration number and mobile number, so we can check you are an SGT student and reach you about the event.

    What data do you keep, and can I delete it?

    Your name, university registration number, mobile number, school, year, email, your consent choices and the poster code you scanned. Your mobile number is never shown on any page, leaderboard or team list. Nothing is stored on your phone. You can ask the organisers to delete your data at any time; the consent form explains how.
