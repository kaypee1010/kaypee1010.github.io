Subject: Revised canteen timings and visitor passes

1. From 1 December 2026, the staff canteen will be open from 8 am to 8 pm on working days. On Sundays it will stay closed.
2. Lunch will be served in two batches: Batch 1 for Blocks A and B, Batch 2 for Blocks C and D. Section heads will share the batch times with their teams.
3. All visitors must collect a paper pass at the main gate and return it when they leave. Passes are valid for one day only.
