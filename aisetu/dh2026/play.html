Basics of Python for Literary Studies

Five levels, five badges, one afternoon. Read, run, guess, detect.

  1. Level 1 Scribe: print, strings, a sentence as data
  2. Level 2 Collector: lists, the lines of a poem, counting
  3. Level 3 Reader: loops, reading a text word by word
  4. Level 4 Archivist: dictionaries, the word frequency of a story
  5. Level 5 Literary Detective: a team guesses the writer of mystery passages from word fingerprints
